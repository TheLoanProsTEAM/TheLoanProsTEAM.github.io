FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your peptides for human use?
No — absolutely not. Every product we sell is labeled and sold strictly for laboratory research use only. They are not for human or veterinary use, administration, injection, or any clinical or personal use of any kind.
What does "8x tested" mean?
Every batch passes eight quality gates: identity and purity by HPLC at synthesis, pre-seal, and post-pack; LC-MS/MS confirmation; endotoxin screening; heavy-metals panel; sterility check; and net-content assay. The full results ship with your order as a COA.
How do I verify the purity of my batch?
Each vial carries a batch serial number. Enter it on our COA lookup (or scan the QR on the box) to download the HPLC chromatogram and mass-spec report for your specific lot.
How are orders shipped?
Lyophilized compounds ship in insulated, temperature-monitored cold-chain packaging within 0–2 business days. Most domestic orders arrive in 2 days; international orders typically within 5–10 business days.
Who can order?
Verified researchers 21 or older, purchasing for lawful in-vitro / laboratory research. Institutional, academic, and biotech accounts may be asked for verification documentation.
What is The Club?
A flexible subscription: any 4 compounds monthly at 20% off with free 2-day cold-chain delivery. Pause, swap compounds, or cancel anytime. One-time orders still earn points — 100 points = $1 at checkout.
What is your return policy?
Due to the sensitive nature of research compounds, opened vials cannot be returned. Unopened, cold-chain-intact items may be returned within 14 days. COA discrepancies are replaced or refunded in full.
